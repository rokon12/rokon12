{%- comment -%} Sort by the full event date when known, otherwise by year. Grouping preserves the original order for talks whose dates are tied or known only to the year. The result is available to the caller as sorted_talks. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign talk_date_groups = include.talks | group_by_exp: 'talk', 'talk.eventDate | default: talk.date' | sort: 'name' -%} {%- if include.descending -%} {%- assign talk_date_groups = talk_date_groups | reverse -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign sorted_talks = '' | split: ',' -%} {%- for talk_date_group in talk_date_groups -%} {%- assign sorted_talks = sorted_talks | concat: talk_date_group.items -%} {%- endfor -%}
